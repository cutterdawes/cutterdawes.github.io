I’m interested in understanding how cognition emerges from (artificial and biological) neural networks at scale, and using that understanding to build open-source AI that empowers people to do amazing things. With a background in mathematics and cognitive science, I am currently working as a member of technical staff at Zyphra to build high-performance neural networks with novel architectures.
